## Shape: ten frames, played in reverse (the plan's shape)

Kannapolis and Newton ran forward in five acts, Flint in six dated days, Sears in seven things it sold. Rochester rewinds like a
roll of film: we open on the bankruptcy (2012) and step back one "frame" at a time to a former bank clerk's box camera (1888).
Every frame shows the effect first and ends on a question that only the earlier frame answers. Epilogue: one whir forward to today,
then the very last image is the beginning — "You press the button, we do the rest."

## The look: the film ages on screen

The ground and type never change (series rule). What changes is the **archive layer**, like a print getting older as we rewind:
frames 10–8 crisp, muted modern colour; 7–6 warm saturated slide colour with a faint halation; 5–4 faded Ektachrome/1963 colour;
3–2 silver black-and-white with grain, gate weave and dust; 1 albumen sepia with vignette and flicker. Grain, weave and flicker
are parameters of the renderer, keyed to the frame number.

## Motion graphics (new for this episode)

- **The rewind card** (signature, between every frame): a 35 mm strip streaks right-to-left with motion blur, slows, and stops on
  one frame; a mechanical exposure counter (Plex Mono digits on drums) rolls down to FRAME 9, the year is stamped in red, the
  frame's title types in. Sound: the rewind whir, plus the score literally rewinding (see Sound).
- **Film strip as layout**: photographs ride on a 35 mm strip with sprocket holes and edge numbers; the strip advances a frame at a
  time with a claw-pull jerk on the shutter sound.
- **Frame 10**: a court filing typesets itself; 604 dots (one per 100 workers) fall away to ~70; two coins on a scale ($23.8M vs ~$1B).
- **Frame 9**: Kodachrome slide mounts flip in; a carousel tray turns; a U.S. map where yellow canisters stream to Parsons, Kansas,
  until the last one lands; the projector lamp goes out.
- **Frame 8**: a line-drawn factory with charges flashing, then collapsing into a particle cloud (schematic, labelled); Kodak Park as
  a field of blocks that empties.
- **Frame 7**: U.S. market-share bars (Kodak 21.9% → #1); a dollar split by margin (film vs digital camera); a phone outline
  swallows a camera; the Dow ticker removes one name.
- **Frame 6**: a workforce counter climbing to 60,400; 9-in-10 / 8-in-10 icon arrays; the Mavica as an outlined floppy.
- **Frame 5**: the 100×100 grid fills pixel by pixel into a picture; a cassette's reels turn for 23 seconds; the patent drawing
  draws itself; a CRT glow shows the picture.
- **Frame 4**: Rochester's population line drawn from 1880 to 2020 (peak marked 1950).
- **Frame 3**: Eastman's gifts as labelled lines radiating from Rochester; Kodachrome's three colour layers peel apart.
- **Frame 2**: the Brownie assembles from parts; a 1912 wage-dividend envelope opens; 1906 factory photos on the strip.
- **Frame 1**: the 1888 patent drawing draws itself; the 100 round exposures fill a grid; the camera travels by mail to Rochester and
  back on a map.

## Sound: a score that rewinds

One theme ("the button theme", a simple, hopeful parlour melody) is the spine of the score, and the music tells the story backwards:
- **Hook and frame 10**: the theme is heard *reversed* — swelling reverse-piano notes and a cold synth pad, solo piano in D minor.
- **Frames 9–7**: soft electric-era colours (vibraphone, synth pad, muted strings); fragments of the theme, never finished.
- **Frames 6–4**: mid-century warmth — strings and vibraphone in major for the peak; it cools after Sasson's camera.
- **Frame 3**: string quartet and piano (Kodachrome was invented by a pianist and a violinist; Eastman built a music school);
  a flute whistles the theme once.
- **Frame 2**: parlour upright with a light ragtime lilt and clarinet (1900s).
- **Frame 1**: music box and solo upright play the theme complete, in F major, for the first time — hopeful, which hurts.
- **Between frames**: the rewind whir, and the last bar of music tape-rewinds (pitch swept down and reversed) into the card.
- **Epilogue**: a fast-forward chirp, then the theme once more, slower, on piano and strings.
Music ducks ≈ 8 dB under the voice; −14 LUFS. Never the Paul Simon song (named only).
